CLUTCH ALARM

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: April 21, 2026
Short version: Clutch Alarm does not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information. The only data stored on our server is an anonymous device token used to send push notifications, and the in-app preferences you configure (favorite teams, players, and alert settings). We use a privacy-first analytics service (TelemetryDeck) to understand how features are used, in aggregate and with no personal identifiers.

1. WHO WE ARE

Clutch Alarm is developed by Itamar Kenan and Nadav Keshet. The idea and Android app were created by Nadav Keshet, and the iOS app was developed by Itamar Kenan. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at feedback@clutchalarm.com.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect only what is strictly necessary to deliver the app's core functionality and to improve it:

  • Device Push Token (FCM Token): A randomly generated, anonymous identifier provided by Google Firebase that allows us to send push notifications to your device. This token is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal data.
  • In-App Preferences: Settings you configure inside the app — including your favorite teams, favorite players, alert thresholds, global radar preferences, and language. These are stored on our server solely to restore your preferences across sessions and to power alerts.
  • Anonymous Usage Signals (TelemetryDeck): Coarse, non-identifying events such as "app launched", "alarm created", "favorite added", "box score viewed", together with generic context like app version, iOS version, device model family, and country. Signals never contain your name, email, push token, team selections, or any other personal data. They are hashed and batched by TelemetryDeck and used by us only to understand which features are useful in aggregate.

We do not collect or store your name, email address, phone number, precise location, payment information, contacts, advertising identifier (IDFA), or any other personally identifiable information.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • To deliver real-time push notifications about NBA games and player performance that match your configured preferences.
  • To save and restore your alert settings and favorites between app sessions.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the app's features.

4. TRACKING, ANALYTICS & COOKIES

Clutch Alarm uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service designed specifically to not track individual users. TelemetryDeck does not use advertising identifiers, tracking pixels, cookies, or cross-app/website profiling. No IDFA is accessed. Signals are anonymous aggregate counters — for example, "an iOS 17 device in Israel opened the app" — and are not tied back to any user account or push token. Because no identifier that persists across apps is ever used, the app does not participate in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

If you use a DNS-level privacy tool (such as NextDNS, Pi-hole, or AdGuard) that blocks *.telemetrydeck.com, analytics signals simply never leave your device. The app continues to work normally.

5. DATA SHARING AND THIRD PARTIES

We do not sell, trade, or rent your information to any third party. The following third-party services are used to operate and improve the app:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging): Used to deliver push notifications. Google may process device identifiers as part of this service. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • 365scores (Public API): We fetch publicly available basketball game data, rosters, and live scores from 365scores for NBA, Euroleague, EuroCup, FIBA Basketball Champions League, and the Israeli Winner League. No user data is sent to 365scores. 365scores Privacy Policy
  • TelemetryDeck (Privacy-First Analytics): Processes anonymous feature-usage signals. TelemetryDeck is a European service (Germany-based), GDPR-compliant by design, and collects no personal identifiers. TelemetryDeck Privacy Policy
  • Railway (Server Hosting): Our backend server is hosted on Railway.app. Preference data stored on our server resides in Railway's infrastructure. Railway Privacy Policy

6. DATA RETENTION

Your push token and preferences are stored on our server for as long as you use the app. Because no account or email is associated with your data, there is no account-deletion flow — your data is identified only by your device's anonymous push token. If you uninstall the app, your token becomes inactive and any associated preferences will no longer be used.

If you wish to have your data deleted, email us at feedback@clutchalarm.com and we will remove your token and preferences from our server within 30 days.

7. PERMISSIONS

Android:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to send push notification alerts.
  • WAKE_LOCK / DISABLE_KEYGUARD: Allows the alarm to wake your screen when a notification arrives.
  • VIBRATE: Enables vibration with alarm notifications.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: Allows notifications to appear on the lock screen in full-screen alarm style.

iOS:

  • Push Notifications: Required to deliver game and player alerts.
  • Background Remote Notifications: Allows the app to process incoming alerts while in the background.

All permissions on both platforms are used solely to deliver alarm notifications. No permission is used to collect personal data.

8. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFER

Our server is hosted on Railway.app and may be located outside your country of residence. By using the app, you consent to the transfer and processing of your anonymous device token and preferences on servers that may be in a different jurisdiction. Since no personally identifiable information is collected, the risk associated with this transfer is minimal.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about your device token.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete all data associated with your device token.
  • Portability: Receive your stored preferences in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.

Since we identify users only by anonymous push token, we may ask you to provide your token (available in the app's Settings screen) to locate your data. To exercise any of these rights, email feedback@clutchalarm.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. GDPR (EUROPEAN USERS)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland:

  • Lawful Basis: We process your anonymous device token and preferences based on legitimate interest — specifically, to deliver the core functionality of the app (push notifications for game alerts). No consent is required because no personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Data Controller: Itamar Kenan and Nadav Keshet are the joint data controllers. Contact: feedback@clutchalarm.com.
  • Automated Decision-Making: The app uses automated logic to determine when to send alerts (e.g., score gap thresholds, player stat conditions). These decisions are based solely on publicly available game data and your configured preferences — no profiling of personal data is involved.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. CCPA (CALIFORNIA USERS)

If you are a California resident:

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes beyond what is needed to provide the app.
  • You have the right to know what data we collect, request deletion, and opt out of sale (though we do not sell data). We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

12. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Clutch Alarm is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. SECURITY

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the data stored on our server. The anonymous nature of our data (no names, emails, or passwords) significantly limits the risk of a data breach affecting your personal information.

14. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify affected users through the app or our website within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by applicable law.

15. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

16. GOVERNING LAW

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Israel. Any disputes arising from this policy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Israel.

17. CONTACT US

If you have any questions, concerns, or data deletion requests, please contact us:

feedback@clutchalarm.com
